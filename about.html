I'm Matthew: a programmer, software developer, and writer. I live in East London where I like to drink coffee, learn languages, read books, take photos, and ride my bike.

This website is about me, not just my work. I don't want the web to be a place for things, I want it to be a place for people.

My surname has an apostrophe in it. This makes me the enemy of many web forms. Notes:

  • I think more about form accessibility more than I otherwise would.
  • I have cultivated a healthy disregard for certain assumed authorities. When a website says you need to enter your name "exactly as it is printed on your [official document]", they are lying.
  • It's interesting to see how a common punctuation mark can make software fall over. Replacing ' with &#39; is the most common mistake, for reasons.

Is it a good domain name? No, but it feels like me.
