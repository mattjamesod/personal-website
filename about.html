Hi, I'm Matthew. I live in London, UK, which is my favourite place on Earth. I grew up in Teesside in the North-East of England, a collection of interconnected towns on the Tees river. From Teesside springs my love of cold beaches, my distate for car-centric infrastructure, and the blank stares of people in London when I try to cheerfully ask "y'alright?".

I work as a software developer for FreeAgent, making accounting software for small businesses here in the UK.

I like languages, urbanism, photography, and modern history. You can browse my library to judge my taste. I like meeting new people, so shoot me an email if you like!
