My Review Philosophy

I think there are five buckets I can reliably put things into:

  1. ★★★★★

    Book was amazing, I don't know how it could be improved.

  2. ★★★★☆

    Book was great, but there was something not quite grabbing me.

  3. ★★★☆☆

    Book was solid.

  4. ★★☆☆☆

    Book was shaky, but I got something out of it.

  5. ★☆☆☆☆

    Book was better as a tree.

People try to write well, and you should be trying to read good books, which means ★★★★☆ should be more common than ★★☆☆☆.

I find the extremes to be rare. You can check my Storygraph stats for up-to-date numbers, but at the time of writing, 9.6% of my reviews are ★★★★★ and just 1.3% are ★☆☆☆☆.
