It is 1811, and Napoleon is ascendent. War engulfs Europe, and the national ambitions of her peoples are on the verge of extinction.

You are the one who will stop Napoleon, and restore hope to the continent.

In a climactic battle, you are nearly killed. Dazed, you limp through the battlefield, unsure if your friends and comrades have already perished. You collapse.

You awake in a warm tent. You crane your neck up from the floor, and to your surprise - Napoleon himself is there! He sits on a gilded throne, seeming vexed by something.

"Ahhh, mon ami, you are awake," he says. "We have much to discuss."

You try to leap to your feet, but you are still weak. You collapse back onto your knees. "We have nothing to discuss," you croak out. "I'm here to stop your cruel imperial ambitions!"

"Ah, but zat is exactly what we are to discuss! You see, I conquer Europe, but not for myself, oh no. I conquer, because I must. Zere is a great conflict, bigger zan you and I, between ze glorious French Nation - liberated by ze Revolution - and all ze others.

"France must expand - it is inevitable. You may stop me now, but it is no use - zere is no way to stop conflict arising between ze peoples of Europe again, in ze future. So I conquer, and I unify under France, so zat war can never again arise."

It is surreal to hear him speak, this figure whom you have thought so much about. His speech is so calm, yet you know his logic to be woefully incorrect - the peoples of Europe are united, against him! They have sent you as their champion.

"But I see you are confused, mon ami," he says, nodding his head sagely. "Yes, because of you - zere are now more options. I may be able to cease my war of expansion."

You perk up. "You'll... stop?"

"Oui, mon ami, oui, perhaps. But first..." he snaps his fingers, and two smartly dressed attendants carry into the room a low table which is placed before you.

On the table are three envelopes. They are sealed in coloured wax: one red, one blue, one green.

"Zese options contain instructions for my Grand Armee, to be dispatched at once, on your selection. You may only choose one.

"Zey are made possible by the device your Coalition Army has been building - zis Catalyst, ze designs for which you found in the tombs of Egypt, after expelling my army zere."

"Yes, we're not really sure what it is or what it does but we set the entire world economy to building it anyway. That part that wasn't fighting you, that is." You stare at the envelopes, and wonder if you never really woke, and this is all a dream.

"Option one," Napoleon says, gesturing to the red envelope. 

"Destroy ze Low Countries, forever. They will sink into ze ocean, and everyone living zere shall perish. With ze Low Countries gone, ze natural battle ground between France and ze nations of Europe shall shrink."

"I... what?!" You cry. "Destroy the Low Countries? But I have friends in the Low Countries! They are a crucial part of our Coaltion, and besides - no nation deserves to be extinguished simply to serve your ego!"

"It is not for my ego, not at all! It is a necessary solution. An imperfect one, oui - but wiz ze Low Contries where zey are, conflict will always reoccur, until Europe itself is no more, and all her people murdered!"

"Wait," you say, increasingly sure that this is some battlefield fever dream, "the Catalyst can destroy the Low Countries?"

"Oui." He nods his head mournfully.

"...how?"

"Option Deux!" He cries, gesturing this time to the blue envelope.

"Ze Low Countries will become forever an inalienable part of France. With her natural borders secure, ze chance of conflict will be reduced greatly, although - no ambitions of ze Belgians or ze Dutch can ever be allowed to stir. Zat is why you must become ze new Emperor of France!"

"Emperor? Me? But - I don't want there to be any Emperor! You've not exactly given the best example of the virtues of Empire."

"Ah, but again, it is necessary."

For some reason, you start to consider the implications of this. "Could I choose the blue envelope, then immediately renounce my title, declare France to be a free republic, and the Low Countries to be independent nations that can do whatever they like?"

"No. You must be Emperor, forever, ruling with ze iron fist! And after you, your children! It is ze only way to ensure peace."

Seeing that he is rather hung up on this inevitable conflict hypothesis, you try to be delicate. "I see... but supposing I were forced to abdicate for some other reason, while lamentable, what would happen then?"

"Abdication - zis is completely impossible." He shrugs. "Choose ze blue envelope, and your family shall rule as tyrants forever. It is just how ze machine works."

"I- how on Earth can that even be done? And how do you know how all this works, anyway? And why haven't you already taken the blue envelope for yourse-"

"Option Trois! Ze green envelope. Zis one- it is my favourite." He looks excited, and begins to pace back and forth in the tent.

"Choose ze green envelope, and all people - men, women, children - everywhere in ze world, even in the most distant corners of Asia, Africa, or ze Pacific - all of zem... shall become French!

"And yet!" He cries, dramatically turning on his heel to pace the other way, "they shall all of zem remain not French! All of their other allegiances, nations, traditions - zey will keep zese also! Yet French zey shall become.

"And ze French! Zey shall remember what it is to be backward, to be small, to be part of ze other fledgling nations of ze Earth. And in that realisation, eternal fraternity shall spring.

"Do you see, mon ami, ze most beautiful working of zis machine? All conflict shall be solved, as surely as if I had conquered all ze people of ze world - yet I can do so wiz no canon, no guns, no long campaign. Peace, forever, under France!"

You've never seen anyone so enthralled and pleased by any prospect before. On the face of your mortal enemy, it is rather offputting. 

"Look, I kind of get the other two envelopes here," you say, "even if I don't understand how such grand designs are to be achieved. But what do you mean everyone will become French? Will they be ruled by you? Why would I want that?"

"No no no, mon ami, it is not about ze governments! All ze kings and queens and prime ministers of ze world will all go unchanged.

"I speak only of the soul, of ze soil, of ze spirit of ze fatherland zat all Frenchmen know! All peoples of ze world will know, what it means to be French."

He looks at you, expectantly.

"Why... and uh, how will that solve anything, exactly?" 

He looks puzzled. "But how could it not! With all people being French, France will have no need to expand - why, I could cease my campaigns at once!"

"Oh," you say, surprised at the concession but more sure than ever that none of this is real. "Well, okay then. That is good to know."

He nods excitedly. "And now - your decision!" He waves an dramatic hand over three envelopes, with a longing glance towards the one with the green seal.

You consider. None of this makes a lick of sense, of course, and you're definitely dying in a ditch somewhere having been hit by an artillery shell. Or maybe the devil has a sense of humour, and this is a strange kind of hell.

You have a thousand questions, but you don't expect useful answers to them. You decide to just play along.

"Well, the green envelope is clearly the best one, from what you've said."

"Ah ha!" Napoleon snatches the green envelope triumphantly. "I shall have it delivered at once! Mon ami, you have made a very good decision!"

"Wait a moment," you say, finally pulling yourself up to stand on two feet. The dizziness you feel when standing could almost be real. "I said the green one is the best one, but honestly, it's not what I came here to do. I want you to stand down your armies, yes, but I don't want everyone to... become everyone else, or whatever it is you said before, in order to do it. People should be free to choose their own destinies and build their own nations - differences between people, even the French, don't have to lead to war and bloodshed. People can live together. We've already built a pretty big coalition fighting you. In fact, wasn't fraternity a big part of what your revolution was meant to be about? It seems like the last few years has naturally been leading towards greater diversity, acceptance, and understanding between people, not... some kind of theory that we're always destined to murder each other."

Napoleon is aghast, staring at you on horror while still clutching the green envelope. "Why - you brute! You swine! You would stand here in the midst of perfection, and choose to reject all my options?!"

"*Your* options? Didn't you say the machine made all these things possible?"

"I've never been so insulted in my life," he pouts. "The options are withdrawn. I will return to conquering Europe now."

He starts walking out of the tent.

"Hold on!" You cry, gripped by a sudden fear that maybe you aren't dying in a ditch, and the world has just taken a very strange, dark turn. "I- I didn't actually say I reject anything! I'll still take the green one!"

But he's already gone.

Over the next twenty years, Napoleon's army conquers the entire world, establishing a brutal dictatorship that fundamentally scars civilisation for the next millenia.

When, eventually, the prospect of freedom, equality and understanding returns to the peoples of a war-torn Earth, they remember you.

"Wow, what a dick that person was," they say. "They should've picked one of the options."
