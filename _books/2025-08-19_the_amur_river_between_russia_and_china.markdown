An 80 year old man falls off a horse in Mongolia and fractures multiple ribs; he does not seek medical attention. Instead, he travels most of the way to the Pacific Ocean. He is arrested and questioned by the police in both Russia and China. Somehow, the vibes are calm and sombre.
