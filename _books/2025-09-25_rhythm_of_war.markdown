<div>My second-favourite Stormlight book, after&nbsp;<em>Words of Radiance</em>. A more emotional and slower read than the previous two entries, <em>Rhythm of War</em> succeeds in narrowing the vast scope that made <em>Oathbringer</em> into a slog. It's a focussed character story like the first two books, but built on top of the expanded world <em>Oathbringer</em> created. I much prefer this approach.<br><br>The themes of the Willshaper order sing in this book, much like the Lightweaver themes do in <em>Words</em>. Even though Venli is far from being the Main Character, the idea of being trapped - physically or mentally - and trying to escape, to find your own freedom, your own path, hit very hard in everyone's arcs.&nbsp;<br><br>Why isn't this a 5 star read? There isn't much to distinguish it from&nbsp;<em>Words</em> in this regard, except that the highs of <em>Words</em> feel higher than those in&nbsp;<em>Rhythm of War</em>. This book makes me cry more consistently, and the character work is probably superior - but I dream about scenes from&nbsp;<em>Words of Radiance</em>.<br><br>If I was a tiny bit less stingy with my fifth star, this book would get it - but it juuuust falls short.</div>
