Contextable

Language-learning flashcards

Download from the App Store here (iPhone only): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/contextable/id1622457817

Why does this app exist?

I speak Russian. Like many Slavic languages, Russian has a very complex set of grammar rules that are retained from Proto-Indo-European, the ancestor of most European, Iranian, and North Indian languages. This means that both verbs and nouns can take many forms depending on how they're used in a sentence.

This is frustrating for a learner who natively speaks a language like English, which, while also Indo-European, long ago stopped bothering with most of those grammar rules. I wnated a way to capture different forms of a word in the same context I discovered them in, to get a better intuitive understanding of how they changed.
