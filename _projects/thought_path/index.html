Thought Path

Write down your thoughts before you forget them

Download from the App Store here (iPhone & iPad only): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thought-path/id6458731090

Why does this app exist?

I used to listen to the Cortex podcast, which was an ongoing conversation about how the hosts worked and lived their lives. On useful habit really stuck with me: whenever you have a thought worth remembering, write it down. It doesn't matter what it is or how clear it is right now, and you should err on the side of writing it anyway in doubt. Later, you can easily salvage any useful ideas.

Thought Path is an app that gives you a text box front and center to write. Put anything in there, and tick off your ideas later. I still use it to this day!
