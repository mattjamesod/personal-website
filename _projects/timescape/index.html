Thought Path

A calendar app that only shows an entire year

Download from the App Store here (iPhone only): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timescape-year-planner/id6744339214

Why does this app exist?

In 2024, I had a big physical calendar on my wall that showed all the days of the years at once. I enjoyed blocking off parts of the grid with my travel, work, and social plans. But it didn't sync to my phone was I was out of the house, and i was limited in how many event scould fit onto a certaon day.

Timescape solves all those problems: one screen, one year. No exceptions. Different calendars are different colours, synced to your main iPhone calendar app.
