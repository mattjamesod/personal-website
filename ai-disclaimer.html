AI Disclaimer

Because we all need one.

  • The illustrator doesn't want it to make images, but uses it to draft an email.
  • The copywriter thinks it shouldn't write copy, but it can probably write code.
  • The programmer wishes it didn't write code, but generates images for a presentation.

The above interpretations are increasingly generous as time goes on, and more and more people let their convenience trump their principles. I am no exception: I am a web programmer, and my industry is one that has mandated the adoption of generative AI in the creation process. I could fight against this, at risk of my employment, my lifestyle, and my convenience. I don't. You should probably judge me for that.

However, I beleive the commodification of the ability to think is morally wrong. Generative AI is a scourge upon our communities, and inhibits our ability to connect with and serve one another.

No generative AI was used in creating this website, nor will it it be for anything I create in future under my own name.
